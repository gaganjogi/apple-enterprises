Apple Enterprises is a retailer of water level controllers, ignition coils and motor starters, based in Mandya, Karnataka.

What we do

Controls that look after the pump and the motor

We supply the control units that sit between the power line and a pump or motor. They switch the pump by water level, start the motor, and protect it when the supply goes wrong.

Water level controllers

Digital and automatic controllers that sense the water level in a tank and switch the pump on and off by themselves, so the tank fills without overflowing and nobody has to watch it.

Motor starters

Direct-on-line auto starters for three-phase motors from 1 HP to 20 HP. The unit starts the motor on its own when power is available.

OLSP relays

Overload and single-phase preventer relays. They cut off the motor when it is overloaded or when one phase of the supply fails, which protects the winding from burning out.

Coils

25 A coils with 415 VAC coil voltage, used in motor starter panels.
